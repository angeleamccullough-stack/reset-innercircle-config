01 / SOUND FIRST

Let's get you listening.

This guide cannot hear your speakers or inspect the host's mix. Choose what is happening; do not change live production settings on guesswork.

I hear people, but not the music

First, confirm how the host is sharing music. It may be a Go Live stream or the host's voice mix.

For a stream, open the intended host's Watch Stream tile and check that stream's mute and volume controls. An unrelated gaming stream is not necessarily the music source.

For a voice mix, tell the host: “I hear your voice, but not the music.” Ask them to check the music send to Discord. Clicking a camera picture does not add missing audio.

Return to Sound Stage and ask the host ↗
I cannot hear anything in Discord

Check that you are connected to the intended voice room and are not deafened. Confirm Discord's output device and volume, then check your computer or headset output.

For Go Live, join the intended stream separately and check its stream volume. A moving speaking indicator is not proof that your speakers are receiving sound.

If two guided checks do not restore audio, stop repeating them and ask the host for help. Confirm when you can actually hear it; leaving the room does not mark it fixed.

My Director Studio scene plays without its music

An original generated take may be video-only even when a soundtrack is saved to the project. In Director Studio, open your project, go to Create, then choose Preview scene with music - free and Play with music.

A preview is not an approved export. Do not buy credits or accept an unsuitable take just to check the sound.

Open Director Studio ↗
I am hosting: what should I check?

Confirm the intended delivery route, voice send, music send, and an actual audience-side listening check. Keep incoming Discord audio out of the outgoing mix to avoid feedback.

Use the staff controls in the RMS Discord assistance flow to record readiness. A route or device change requires another check. These records are host/listener confirmations, not automatic measurements.

Do not restart OBS, reconnect audio devices, or play test tones during someone else's performance. Recording requires disclosure and participant consent.
