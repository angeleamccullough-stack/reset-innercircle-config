CLEAR BEFORE YOU COMMIT

What happens next?

After checkout

Stripe confirms your payment. Reset uses the contact and project details supplied at checkout to coordinate the agreed next steps. Payment confirmation is separate from production scheduling and delivery.

What does the deposit purchase?

A deposit toward the approved service scope. It does not purchase a subscription, Discord membership, library access, a copyright transfer or a guaranteed placement.

Revisions, refunds and cancellation

Your approved written project or event agreement controls these terms. Review them before paying. If terms or availability are not confirmed, request a quote first.

Projects below the checkout minimum

Ask Reset for a separate agreed payment arrangement. Do not overpay a deposit to purchase a smaller service.

Your payment information

Enter payment details only in Stripe checkout. Reset never asks you to post card details or passwords in Discord.
